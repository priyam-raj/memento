August 2024. Refused. T-shirt, my own name in the company field, one question. It was early morning, and I'm not a morning person. I'd flown in from Bangalore, so I couldn't even be sad in peace: there was a hotel to check out of and a flight home to catch. Somewhere over Andhra Pradesh, with a passport that had nothing new in it, the only thought was why?

January 2025. Approved. Same consulate, same window.

One interview means two visits to the consulate city: biometrics first, then the actual interview. Two attempts meant four trips to Hyderabad for me. This is what I changed in between.
