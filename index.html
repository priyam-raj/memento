03The record

  1. 2026

    I launch TypeVoice. The typed application is dead; people apply by speaking. typevoice.ai ↗

  2. 2025

    Vid.AI gets ranked among the top 20 highest-revenue startups globally on TrustMRR. Across my products, I've crossed $1M+ a year in revenue. TrustMRR ↗ Ishan Sharma ↗

  3. 2024–

    I'm building Vid.AI with my co-founder Matt. Our users' videos have crossed billions of views combined, and the product got a surprise cameo in a John Oliver segment on HBO. HBO ↗

  4. Mar 2024

    My acquisition tweet ends up on a Times Square billboard.

  5. Jan 2024

    Morise.ai gets acquired. I'm 21, still in college.

  6. 2023

    Timenite gets acquired by CenterPoint Gaming. I work with them for a few months, into 2024.

  7. 2022–23

    I build Morise.ai, AI software for YouTube creators, from my college room. Users from Disney, CoinDCX, Sidemen, MrBeast. It does well enough that I sponsor my own university's fest with it. Ishan Sharma ↗ the ₹1 crore story ↗

  8. 2020

    I start B.Tech CSE at BIT Mesra. The YouTube channel keeps compounding on the side: 18,000 subscribers by 2021, 22,000 today.

  9. 2019

    I build timenite.com. It goes on to pull millions of views. Also shipping iOS apps, self-taught, before engineering college can get to it.

  10. 2016

    My first YouTube uploads, at 100 subscribers. First domain, first AdSense payout. A website could make real money. Noted.

  11. 2011–14

    Little films, a skateboard, and a remote-controlled airplane I built from scratch at twelve. The instinct was already there: make something, show somebody.
